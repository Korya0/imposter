# Product

## Register

product

## Users
Arabic-speaking groups of friends and families during lively social gatherings. They play locally by passing a single mobile device around, needing high legibility, clear visual states, and robust interactive elements that function under diverse lighting and noisy social settings.

## Product Purpose
An offline local multiplayer social deduction game. The app guides players through setting up, assigning secret roles (the imposter vs. group members) via fingerprint check, coordinating discussion timers, and revealing results. Success is measured by how smoothly the phone flows between players and the laughter, mystery, and engagement it fosters.

## Brand Personality
* **Sketchy & Raw**: Embraces hand-drawn, paper-cutout, and rugged cardboard textures.
* **Mysterious & Playful**: A charismatic, detective-like atmosphere mixed with lighthearted, cartoon-like humor.
* **Culturally Elegant**: Grounded in classic Arabic heritage through the artistic `ArefRuqaa` calligraphic typography, contrasted against deep shadows.

## Anti-references
* **SaaS Cliché**: Flat, sterile, clean vector shapes and modern neutral grays that make it feel like a spreadsheet tool.
* **Neon Cyberpunk**: Glowing neon on pure dark, which strips away the physical, organic paper-and-sketch feel.
* **Over-engineered Menus**: Dense forms and tiny inputs that hinder casual, rapid gameplay.

## Design Principles
1. **Physical Cardboard Tactility**: Every UI element must feel like a tangible, hand-cut paper block. Buttons, cards, and bottom sheets should float, indent, or wiggle with organic, physics-backed animations and responsive haptics.
2. **Social-Pass Fluidity**: Visual elements, text sizes, and buttons must be large, high-contrast, and immediately legible from a distance of several feet as the phone is passed around in active environments.
3. **Elegant Imposture**: Establish a distinct personality by combining the high-class calligraphic elegance of `ArefRuqaa` typography with rugged, raw hand-drawn borders and stark, gold-on-black contrasts.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
* **High Contrast**: Perfect readability on a pure black background using vivid golden tints and bright white.
* **Huge Touch Targets**: Large padding and generous tap areas to prevent accidental clicks when passing the device.
* **No Motion Barriers**: Avoid heavy fullscreen screen-shakes; keep animations localized, fluid, and toggleable or naturally integrated to avoid player fatigue.
* **Right-to-Left Native Layout**: Complete Arabic alignment, ensuring standard typography scales and RTL padding are structurally perfect.
